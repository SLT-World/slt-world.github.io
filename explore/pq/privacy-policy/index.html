PQ Privacy Policy

The privacy policy of PQ.

Last updated: 13/9/2026

By accessing or using PQ, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the service.

When we say "PQ," "we," "us," and "our" in these terms, we mean the PQ project operated under the name SLT Softwares by Schuyler Tan.

When we say "you" or "your," we mean you. If you're accessing our services on behalf of a legal entity (like your employer), you agree that you have the authority to bind that entity to these terms, and "you" and "your" will refer to that entity.

settings

Information We Collect

PQ itself does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information.

PQ does not collect your bank details, credit card numbers, or real money transaction data.

However, PQ periodically retrieves currency exchange rate data from its own servers.

settings

Data Transmission

PQ:

  • Does not upload personal files to its own servers.
  • Does not include analytics, tracking, or advertising systems.

However:

  • Network requests are made to its own servers to retrieve currency exchange rates.
  • These requests are necessary for the app to function.
settings

Local Data Storage

PQ may store certain preferences locally on your device, such as:

  • Selected currencies.
  • App settings.

This data is:

  • Stored only on your device.
  • Not transmitted to any external servers by the app.

Children

PQ does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13.
