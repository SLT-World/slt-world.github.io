Information We Collect

Power Station Inc collects information when you download and use it.

This information may include information such as:

  • Your device's location.
  • Pages of the application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages.
  • Time spent on the game.
  • Operating system of the device.

Power Station Inc makes use of the information in the following ways:

  • Geolocation Services: Location data is utilized to provide features such as personalized content, relevant recommendations, and location-based services.
  • Analytics: Aggregated and anonymized location data is used to assist in analyzing user behavior, identify trends, and improve the overall performance and functionality of the application.
  • Third-Party Services: Periodically, the application may transmit anonymized location data to external services. These services can enhance the application and optimizing their offerings.

For a better experience, while using the application, the application may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information, including but not limited to user identity, age, and scores.
